QA

Two playbooks cover most of my day: a check that fails every time, and a test that fails only sometimes.

Hard fail

fail-check-analyzer

Classify product vs test vs environment. Quote the first real error. Propose the next confirmation step — then stop for review.

Intermittent

flaky-test-analyzer

Rank race, data, and order issues. No longer blind sleeps as the “fix”. Quarantine only after a person agrees.

One repo, two areas. QA first — that is the main story. Engineering helpers share the same folder pattern so they stay easy to review.

Human review is required

  • Playbooks draft analysis. They do not mute checks or quarantine tests on their own.
  • Every QA report ends with an explicit “awaiting human review” block.
  • Action happens only after a person accepts the verdict.
